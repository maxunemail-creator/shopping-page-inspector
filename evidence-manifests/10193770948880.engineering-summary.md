## Structural claims visible in detail images

- Two storage shelves plus pegboard integrated into the desk frame (images 6–8, 12).
- Horizontal reinforcing beam across the desk body (images 14 and 18).
- Thickened steel-tube frame is claimed to be about 70% thicker than the ordinary version, but **no absolute tube cross-section or wall thickness is stated** (image 17).
- Side frame uses triangulated/X-style bracing and a 55 cm floor footprint (images 15, 19, 20, 29).
- The marketing images explicitly claim a raised desk-leg/open under-desk layout and show 75 cm desk height (image 20).

## Still unsupported — do not infer

The captured product page does **not** give a reliable numeric value for:

- left/right leg inset from the 140 cm desktop ends;
- clear width between the two leg frames;
- exact pegboard **width**;
- rear crossbar height/offset relative to floor or desktop;
- steel tube width × depth or wall thickness;
- exact rear desktop-edge free depth available to a C-clamp monitor arm;
- clamp clearance between the desktop underside and rear frame/pegboard structure;
- exact shelf-board thickness;
- exact shelf usable width between uprights.

These remain seller/manual/physical-measurement items unless a later source supplies explicit dimensions.
